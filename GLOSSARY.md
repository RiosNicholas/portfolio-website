# Portfolio

Nicholas Rios's personal site: who he is, what he's built, and what's been written by or about him. Content is edited by one admin.

## Profile

**Profile**:
The editable prose about him shown across the site: his intro, summary, availability status, location, and the short introduction on each page.
_Avoid_: Bio, copy, site settings

**CV entry**:
One dated line of background on the About page, belonging to exactly one CV section.
_Avoid_: Resume item, timeline item

**CV section**:
A heading that groups CV entries: Experience, Education, Activities, or Awards & certifications.
_Avoid_: Category (in prose)

**Resume**:
The downloadable PDF of the full CV, served from the site itself.
_Avoid_: CV (when meaning the PDF)

**Skill**:
A practice or discipline he's known for, shown in the home page skills list.

**Tool**:
A specific product he uses and recommends, shown as a favorite tool.
_Avoid_: Skill (for products)

**Language**:
A spoken language and his level in it.

**Recommendation**:
A quote from someone he's worked with, with a link to their profile.
_Avoid_: Endorsement (LinkedIn uses that for one-click skill votes), testimonial

## Work

**Case study**:
A project write-up on the Projects page, reachable by its own anchor rather than its own page.
_Avoid_: Work item, project page

## Writing

**Article**:
A piece he writes and publishes from admin, read on its own page on this site.
_Avoid_: Post, blog

**Mention**:
Something published elsewhere that he wrote or that features him, such as a LinkedIn post or a news story. It is shown as an embed when its source allows it, otherwise as a link with a title.
_Avoid_: Press item, external post

**Source**:
Where a Mention is published (LinkedIn, X, YouTube, a news site). It is recognized from the Mention's URL, not typed.
_Avoid_: Outlet, source tag, platform

**Topic tag**:
A free label for what an Article or Mention is about (design systems, AI), used to filter the Writing page.
_Avoid_: Source tag, category
